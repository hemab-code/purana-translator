### Shiva Purana - The Story of Creation (Part 1) *Narrated by Nemani Suryanarayana* Long ago, during the golden age of the world, there was a deeply spiritual forest called Naimisharanya. It was a sacred sanctuary where the Divine Mother, Sri Lalitambika, had performed intense meditation. At one time, a group of wise sages gathered and prayed to Brahma, the Creator. They asked him to guide them to a divine place where they could perform their spiritual practices in absolute peace—a sanctuary completely untouched by worldly disasters and safe from negative forces. Brahma prayed to Lord Vishnu and created a divine wheel of cosmic energy. He handed it to the sages and said, "Follow this wheel. Wherever its spinning rim finally comes to rest, that will be the most auspicious and peaceful place for your meditation." The divine wheel spun across all the worlds, eventually arriving at a beautiful spot on Earth. There, in the blink of an eye, it destroyed a demon, and the outer rim (*nemi*) of the wheel came to a complete stop. Because the wheel's rim stopped there, this sacred forest came to be known as *Naimisharanya*. Realizing that this was also the very ground where the Divine Mother had once meditated, the joyous sages praised the Creator and happily made Naimisharanya their spiritual home. About eighty-eight thousand sages built their quiet hermitages there, performing sacred rituals and prayers for the well-being of the entire world. One day, the master storyteller Sage Suta arrived at their forest. Suta was the son of the great sage Romaharshana, a classmate of the wise Shuka, and a beloved disciple of Vyasa, the compiler of the Vedas. His arrival brought immense joy to Sage Shaunaka and all the other hermits living in the forest. The sages warmly welcomed Sage Suta, washed his feet, offered him a comfortable seat, and gathered around him with deep respect. Shaunaka then spoke on behalf of everyone: "O great soul! Out of your infinite kindness, you have blessed this forest with your presence. Every time we are fortunate enough to meet you, our hearts yearn to hear the ancient, beautiful stories of the divine, and you have always graciously shared them with us. You are like a deep, boundless ocean of wisdom, effortlessly explaining the subtlest secrets of righteousness. We pray that you bless us today by sharing a divine Purana." Sage Suta closed his eyes for a moment, contemplating a profound question: *What is the one sacred scripture that can uplift and save humanity from the suffering, chaos, and confusion of the modern age?* As he meditated on the three-eyed Lord Shiva, the glorious *Shiva Purana* flashed vividly in his heart. Opening his eyes, Suta looked at the gathering of eager sages with a warm, gentle smile and loving eyes. "My dear friends," he said, "I am overjoyed to share with you the magnificent Shiva Purana! It is the most beautiful and exalted of all scriptures, the giver of wisdom and ultimate liberation. One can only have the fortune of hearing this divine story as a result of good deeds accumulated over many lifetimes. All happens by the will of Shiva!" With these words, Sage Suta first meditated upon his master, the great Vyasa, and began the narration of this grand scripture with a beautiful hymn of praise to Lord Shiva, originally sung by Vyasa himself: *** #### Vyasa’s Hymn of Praise to the Lord of Grace *I bow to the Supreme God, the Lord of all lords, who holds the mighty trident;* *Salutations to Him who is three-eyed, the divine master of all yogis!* *I bow to the God of gods, the Great Creator, the source of all peace,* *The eternal, unchanging pillar of light, the Auspicious One, the Supreme Soul!* *Salutations to Him who is with the Divine Mother, the fierce Rudra, the ultimate dissolver of the cosmos;* *I seek refuge in the one with the unusual eyes, the ultimate shelter of all spiritual seekers.* *To the Great Lord, the master of all unions, the sovereign ruler and consort of Ambika,* *The ultimate yogi, the giver of yoga, who wraps Himself in the cosmic mystery of Maya.* *To the Master and Teacher of all yogis, who is reached through deep meditation, the eternal one,* *The fierce Rudra who ferries us across the ocean of worldly existence, the Lord of cosmic wisdom.* *The eternal, the all-pervading peace, the supreme consciousness, beloved of the wise,* *The one with matted locks, the embodiment of all arts, the formless Lord of the immortals.* *The One, yet appearing in grand forms, known only through sacred wisdom, the ultimate destination of the righteous,* *The blue-throated Lord, the cosmic form, who pervades everything as the seed of creation.* *He who is the fire of time, the destroyer of death, the source of love, and the destroyer of worldly desire,* *I bow to the Lord of the sacred mountains, whose crown is adorned with the beautiful crescent moon.* *The three-eyed Lord, radiant like the sun, the supreme ruler,* *The fierce protector of all living beings, the brilliant light that shines beyond all darkness!* *** After singing this beautiful hymn of praise, Sage Suta said, "O noble sages! Within this very hymn lies hidden the eight-fold cosmic forms of Lord Shiva. I shall now explain their great significance to you. Listen with an open heart and deep attention!"
